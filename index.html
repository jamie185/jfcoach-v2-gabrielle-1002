first on open.

Food is never the first or biggest thing on Home any more.

Samsung Health
connects again.

We found why your Galaxy couldn't connect, and it's fixed.

  • S1 · Health Connect fixed

    JF Coach was asking Android phones for heart rate and distance, which the store app no longer lists. Android 13 then quietly refused everything, even though Health Connect showed JF Coach as allowed.

    It now asks only for what it needs, and one refused item no longer stops the rest syncing.

  • Clear next steps on screen

    If something's still off, the Samsung Health screen says exactly what, with an Open Health Connect button and the steps underneath.

    On iPhone, if a client didn't allow Apple Health access, it says so plainly and links to Health permissions.

  • Water from Apple Health and Health Connect

    Ready and waiting. It switches on with the next App Store and Play Store update. The Water screen will then show "Includes 1,500 ml from Apple Health". Today's app works exactly as before.

What to tell Samsung clients

  1. Fully close JF Coach and open it again.Swipe it away from recent apps so it loads the fix.
  2. More > Connected apps > Health Connect > Connect
  3. If it asks: Open Health Connect > App permissions > JF Coach > Allow all
  4. In Samsung Health: Settings > Health Connect, and turn sharing on for steps and exercise.Steps then come through on their own.

The right club,
easy to change.

No more guessed clubs. Clients pick, trainers can correct.

  • K1 · A client's club fixed

    The client who trains at Kalkallo now shows at Kalkallo. Her club had never been saved, so the map guessed one. That guessing is gone for everyone.

  • Clients without a club are asked to choose

    They see "Choose your club" on the Challenge tab, the Clubs board and the map.

    Everyone already in the challenge now has a club: from their own sign-up or HubSpot where we had it, otherwise our best guess (their trainer's club). If any look wrong, change it from the client's profile, or the client can change it themselves.

    Clients: Edit your answers (Home or More), or the Choose your club row. Trainers and admins: the client's profile > Spring Kickstart > Club.

Habits that stay put
and make sense.

We found what kept removing habits, fixed it and put yours back.

  • H3 · Your habits are back

    Walk outside is back to every day, and Recovery stretch is back on Monday, Wednesday and Friday.

    The cause: redoing the setup form swapped habits when the goal changed, and rebuilt daily habits as Monday to Friday. Both fixed. We checked every client: nobody else lost a habit this way.

  • H2 · "Log every session" ticks itself

    It's gone from the suggestions. Clients who already have it see it tick on its own when they finish a workout.

  • H4 · Every habit says what to do

    One plain line under each habit, e.g. Push power: "Do a set of push-ups today, as many as you can with good form."

    Tap the (i) to see what it asks, its days and points, with Mark Done and Change Your Habits.

Health Score points
that add up.

Each suggestion shows what that one thing really earns.

  • H5 · Honest +points

    "Catch up on missed workout" no longer claims the whole gap (+50). Each action shows what doing it adds, e.g. +9 for today's workout.

    Meal asks say exactly what they mean: "Log 7 meals this week, 4 done so far". Meals are worth a little more than before.

Secret challenges
that play fair.

Famous places, warmer or colder on every search, and a proper celebration.

  • C1 · The MCG and friends

    The MCG now leads the sporting clues, with famous spots like Federation Square, Eureka Skydeck, the State Library, Melbourne Zoo and Cook's Cottage. Sydney clients get Sydney landmarks.

  • C2 · New mix, for all ages

    The "school girls" clue is gone and the deli hall clue no longer mentions cheese. Clues cycle easy, fun, sporting, historical, starting in the client's own city.

  • C3 · Second clue after 3 misses

    At trainer clubs too. It used to appear on first open. The clue sheet counts down: "a second clue unlocks after 2 more searches that miss".

  • C4 · Warmer or colder, every time

    Every search compares with the last one. Warmer is orange, colder is blue. Inside 1 km: Hotter (red) or Cooling off (teal).

  • C5 · "So close, zoom in"

    Zoomed out but right over the spot says "So close. It's right around here. Zoom in and search again." (purple). Zoomed right out over a huge area says "Zoom in first".

  • C6 · Celebrations

    Confetti, congratulations and points that pop and fly into the JF points total. A find pays +50 straight away, and finishing the task pays +200.

    Same moment for all habits ticked, a full week of workouts and the weekly challenge. Health Score shows only when it actually moved, and each one shows once, right after it happens.

Build Program,
the client's way.

More choices back, old programs reusable, and no repeated Back taps.

  • B1 · Use an old program again

    Previous programs in Build and in Training > Manage program. Tap Use Again and it becomes the current one. The one it replaces is saved there too.

  • B2 · Never overrides by accident

    If a client chose to keep their program, nothing in the form can replace it. The server checks this too.

  • B3 · Keep or build, at the top

    It's the first question in the form, with "Keep my program" ticked by default. Skipping through can't override.

  • B4 · Resumes at question 1

    Coming back to a saved build starts at the first build question, with Rebuild Now.

  • B5 · More choices back

    Training type (full body, upper/lower, push/pull/legs, five-day split, glute focus), weeks, focus, equipment and a note for JFI.

The form, easy
to find and safe.

Keep, build now or build later, then a summary before anything changes.

  • O1 · On Home and More

    "Edit your answers" (or "Finish your setup") is on Home and in More, as well as Challenge.

  • O2 · Three clear choices

    Keep my current program, Build one now, or Build one later. Only "later" with no current program gets a recommended one.

  • O3 · Clients choose the split

    "Build one now" asks for the type, length and equipment. No more automatic full body.

  • O4 · Back where they started

    Finishing the form returns to the page it was opened from.

  • O5 · Summary first

    "Check your plan" lists every answer with Change. If the program will be replaced, an orange note says so first. The old one is saved in Training under Previous programs.
